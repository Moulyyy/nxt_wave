# Layer 2: Track A — Top-of-Funnel | New Avenue

## The Avenue: AI-Powered "Career DNA" Assessment Engine

**What it is:** A free, shareable, 2-minute AI-powered career assessment tool that intercepts 12th-grade students at their peak decision-making anxiety — right after board results. The student inputs their board scores, interests, and preferred language. An AI engine generates a hyper-personalized "Career DNA Report" — visual, sharable (Instagram story-ready), multilingual — that maps them to a specific tech career path and shows their earning potential trajectory. The report ends with a personalized NIAT campus recommendation and a one-click NAT registration CTA.

**Why NIAT isn't running it:** NIAT's current top-of-funnel is either paid (performance ads) or manual (campus activations, counsellor networks). They have no self-propagating, zero-marginal-cost viral instrument. Every lead costs either media spend or human hours. This is a product-led growth play — the assessment IS the marketing, and sharing it IS the distribution.

## Why It Fits NIAT Specifically

- **35+ campus partners = personalized routing.** Unlike Scaler (one campus), NIAT can match a student to their nearest/best-fit partner university — the assessment becomes a location-aware recommendation engine, not just a generic quiz.
- **Vernacular-first DNA.** NIAT already supports Hindi/Telugu learners. A multilingual assessment in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada instantly reaches parents who can't parse English-only edtech funnels. Competitors don't do this.
- **Board result timing = intent spike.** 12th results drop May-June. That's a 4-6 week window where 15M+ students and their parents are actively Googling "what to do after 12th." NIAT currently fights for this attention with ads. The assessment tool would own this moment organically.

## Hypothesis

**If** we launch the Career DNA tool 1 week before CBSE results with vernacular support and Instagram-story sharing, **then** we'll generate 50,000+ organic assessments in the first 30 days, converting 8-12% into NAT registrations (4,000-6,000 high-intent leads). **Why:** Similar career quiz tools (16Personalities, Holland Code tests) show 15-30% share rates and 2-5 minute session times. With board-result anxiety as the trigger, a visual shareable output, and WhatsApp/Instagram native sharing, we expect higher virality. Cost per lead: near-zero after build.

## The ONE Experiment

**Scope:** Build a working MVP of the Career DNA tool with 3 languages (English, Hindi, Telugu). Run a 14-day pilot targeting AP/Telangana state board result week.

**Execution:** Deploy the assessment on a subdomain (careerdna.niatindia.com). Seed it via 5 micro-influencers (₹2K each = ₹10K total budget) who post their own Career DNA results as Instagram stories with "Just got my result — here's what AI says my career should be" framing. Add a WhatsApp share button that auto-formats the result as a shareable message.

**Timeline:** Build in 5 days. Seed on result day. Measure for 14 days.

## How I'd Know It Worked

**Primary Metric:** Viral coefficient (K-factor) > 1.0 — each user brings in more than 1 additional user through sharing.

**Secondary Metric:** Assessment-to-NAT-registration conversion rate > 8%.

**Threshold:** If K-factor stays below 0.7 after 7 days, the sharing mechanic needs redesign. If conversion stays below 5%, the NIAT CTA placement/messaging needs rework. Kill if both fail after 14 days.
